LLM:
Large language models (LLMs) are advanced AI systems that understand and generate natural language, or human-like text, using the data they’ve been trained

token:
A token is the smallest unit of text an AI system uses to interpret and generate language, and it can represent a full word, part of a word, a character, or even a short phrase.

context window:
A context window is the maximum amount of text—measured in chunks called tokens—that an artificial intelligence model can process, see, and "remember" at any single time
